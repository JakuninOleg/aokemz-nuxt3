# Stop-slop references (compact)

Adapted from [hardikpandya/stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop). Prefer rewriting the sentence, not blind deletion.

## Punctuation tells

| Avoid | Prefer |
|-------|--------|
| Em dash `—` | `.` `,` `:` `()` or split sentence |
| Dash chains `A – B – C` as style | One clear relation |
| Dramatic `…` | Period or cut |

## Phrases to cut (EN)

it's worth noting, here's what, in today's world, delve into, landscape, robust, seamless, leverage, unlock, empower, journey, tapestry, testament, nestled, vibrant

## Phrases to cut (RU)

важно отметить, следует подчеркнуть, в современном мире, комплексный подход, уникальное предложение, высокий уровень, динамично развивающийся, позволяет обеспечить

## Structures to avoid

- Binary contrast: «не просто X, а Y» / «not X, but Y» → state Y
- Negative listing: «Not a blog. Not a forum. A…» → name the thing
- False agency: «the platform decides» → who decides
- Meta: «In this section we will…» → just start
- Staccato slogan stacks under every H2

## Keep

Domain terms (ДПЭ, 4ГПЭ, ЭКГ, УХЛ), quotes from real docs, honest uncertainty («по каталогу», «по открытым данным»).
